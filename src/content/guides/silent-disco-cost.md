Silent disco pricing comes down to two questions: how many headsets you need and whether you want a DJ. Everything else is small.

## Rental only

Our rental-only package starts at $500 and includes headsets, transmitters, delivery, setup, and pickup. You bring the music: a DJ, a band, or a phone with three playlists. The final number scales with headcount and date.

## Rental plus DJ

Starts at $1,300 and adds a professional DJ who programs and runs all three channels, brings a mic, and stays for the event. For most weddings this is the better value, because it replaces a separate DJ booking.

## Party favor upgrade

Glow sticks, LED glasses, and light-up extras for every guest. It's an add-on to either package and turns the dance floor into the photo everyone posts.

## What moves the price

- **Quantity.** The biggest factor. See [how many you need](/guides/how-many-headphones-do-i-need/).
- **Date.** Saturdays in wedding season and December book first. Off-peak dates have more room.
- **Distance.** Delivery in the Mobile Bay area is included. Farther out may add travel.
- **Hours.** DJ packages are priced for a typical reception. Longer events are quoted.
- **Extras.** The party favor upgrade, a second mic, or an attendant for headset distribution.

## How to keep it down

1. Count honestly. Rent for the dance floor, not the guest list.
2. Bring your own music if you already have a DJ or a playlist you love.
3. Pick a Friday or Sunday if your date is flexible.
4. Book early. Late bookings pay for rush logistics.

## Compared to a regular DJ

A silent disco with our DJ costs about what a good traditional DJ costs, with headsets included. If you already booked a DJ, the rental-only package is the cheaper route: our transmitters plug straight into the DJ's mixer.
